Building Juptee

How Joyonto Rozario built a clothing and custom T-shirt brand.

Juptee is a Bangladesh-based clothing, customized T-shirt and e-commerce brand founded by Joyonto Rozario. The business combines apparel, custom design and direct-to-customer e-commerce into one brand experience.

The goal was not simply to sell T-shirts. I wanted Juptee to feel like a recognizable lifestyle brand with its own visual language, product stories and community.

As founder, I work across the business — from visual identity and product concepts to digital design, marketing, customer experience and e-commerce decisions.

Juptee E-commerce Experience

Designing for the moment between “I like it” and “I’ll order it.”

Juptee operates as a direct-to-customer e-commerce business, so design decisions have to work beyond aesthetics. Product hierarchy, pricing communication, delivery information, trust, customization and campaign messaging all influence the buying experience.

Christian T-Shirt Campaigns

Jesus and Christian-themed T-shirts for Easter, Christmas and everyday faith.

Some of Juptee's most recognizable products are Christian and Jesus-themed T-shirts. These collections are especially relevant around Easter Sunday and the Christmas season, while remaining available throughout the year for customers who want faith-inspired apparel.

Campaigns are built around the occasion rather than only the product: the creative direction, message, imagery and offer work together to make the collection feel timely.

Easter

Everyday faith

Joyonto Rozario — My Role

“I wasn’t only designing Juptee. I was building the business around the brand.”

As founder, I have been involved across the full creative and commercial process: brand direction, graphic design, UI/UX and website thinking, product concepts, apparel artwork, campaign design, social media creatives, customer experience, marketing and day-to-day business decisions.

With a small three-person team, the work is highly hands-on. That means moving quickly, testing ideas, learning from customers and turning successful design directions into new products.
